SkyDisplay Weather Screen

Proposed third screen for the WT32-SC01 Plus, fed by Weather Underground a personal weather station. Swipe order on the device: forecast, weather, status. The values shown are examples, not live readings.

Amber marks something that matters for the rigs: dew risk, gusts, or a stale station.

What's on the screen

  • Outside now: temperature, feels-like, change over the last hour, and a 24-hour temperature and dew point trace.
  • Dew point and spread: the gap between temperature and dew point. Under 3 °C it turns amber: dew heaters on.
  • Wind: speed, gust and direction. Gusts over 30 km/h turn amber.
  • Pressure with its 3-hour trend, plus rain today.
  • Cloud tonight: from the Weather Underground forecast, as a second opinion next to the Clear Sky Chart.
  • Five-day strip: tonight and the next four days, with highs, lows, chance of precipitation and night cloud cover.

Polling, within the free key's limits

  • Current observations every 5 minutes: 288 calls a day.
  • Last 24 hours (for the trace and trends) every 15 minutes: 96 a day.
  • 5-day forecast every hour: 24 a day.
  • About 410 calls a day in total. A personal weather station key allows 1,500 a day and 30 a minute.
  • Station ID, API key and units go on the settings page. The key is stored on the device and never shown in full.

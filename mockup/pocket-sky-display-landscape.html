Pocket Sky Display

A smaller take on the Bedside Sky Display, for Seeed's 1.47" XIAO ESP32-S3 Plus touch board (320×172, landscape). It uses the same Clear Sky Chart data (a sample site, fetched Sat 14 Nov 2026) and the same scoring. Tap the screen to switch between the glance view and tonight's hour-by-hour view.

The board

Screen1.47" IPS, 172 × 320, JD9853A driver, capacitive touch
ChipESP32-S3R8: 8 MB PSRAM, 16 MB flash
Extras6-axis IMU, microphone, microSD, 2 buttons, LiPo connector
Size26.4 × 51.4 × 12.6 mm

What changes from the big screen

  • The three tiles stay side by side, each with a verdict and two short detail lines.
  • The strip shows 48 hours: the score and the average of the three cloud models, plus darkness. The individual model rows move to the hourly view.
  • The hourly view runs tonight's dark hours left to right, with RDPS, ECMWF and HRRR cloud (R E H) and transparency (T) as rows, like a zoomed-in Clear Sky Chart.
  • With 8 MB of PSRAM, the whole chart GIF fits in memory easily.
  • The IMU could switch views when the device is lifted or tapped, and turn the screen off when it's laid face down.

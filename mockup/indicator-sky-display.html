Indicator Sky Display

The sky forecast display on Seeed's SenseCAP Indicator D1: a 4" square 480×480 touchscreen in a finished desk case. It uses the same Clear Sky Chart data (a sample site, fetched Sat 14 Nov 2026) and the same scoring as the Bedside and Pocket mockups.

Visible area about 72 × 72 mm. The square screen fits the full chart rows and a reminder panel.

What the extra space adds

  • The chart keeps every sky row from the Clear Sky Chart: RDPS, ECMWF and HRRR cloud, transparency, seeing and darkness, plus a score row. It covers 48 hours.
  • The tiles get room for a second detail line, so "models split" and the transparency are always visible.
  • A reminder panel uses the built-in buzzer: a short chime at 16:00 when tonight is GO, so there's time to set up the rigs. Tap the panel to silence it.
  • The ground panel shows tonight's highest humidity, with a dew warning at 85% or more, and the calmest wind.

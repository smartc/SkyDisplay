Bedside Sky Display

WT32-SC01 Plus (480×320), drawn from the Dark Dragons Clear Sky Chart for a sample site as fetched Sat 14 Nov 2026. Drag the time slider to see how the three status lights change through the forecast.

Actual size on the device: 73 × 49 mm visible area

How the lights decide

  • Hour quality. Average the cloud models available for that hour (RDPS, ECMWF, HRRR). Good is ≤ 20% cloud with transparency Average or better. Fair is ≤ 40% with Below Average or better. Seeing is ignored for deep sky.
  • Night window. The run of dark-navy cells in the Darkness row. Twilight and moonlit cells don't count.
  • GO: at least 4 good hours in a row.
  • MAYBE: a 2–3 hour good run, or 4+ good-or-fair hours.
  • NO: anything less.
  • Confidence. When the models differ by 40% or more, the tile says the models disagree.
  • Now grades the current hour after dark. In daylight it's grey and says when dark starts.

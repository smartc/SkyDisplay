Observatory screen

A fourth SkyDisplay screen: roof, safety, sky conditions, mount park sensors and each rig's imaging state at a glance. Sample data, drawn at the real screen sizes with the device's fonts and colours.

Scenario
Rigs configured With more than two rigs the list shows two at a time, turns the page every 5 s, and a tap on the screen turns it now.

Where the data comes from

ShownSource
RoofAlpaca Dome: shutterstatus, atpark; error text from the device
SafetyOne Alpaca SafetyMonitor gives the verdict (here Llama Watch). When it says unsafe, Llama Watch also sends Comments.UnsafeMonitors (monitor, reason, value, threshold); the first one is shown as the reason
RainWet or dry from the rain sensor itself: its own SafetyMonitor, or its UDP safety broadcast. Shown separately, since the verdict can be unsafe for other reasons
Cloud, SQM, lux, sky tempAlpaca ObservingConditions: cloudcover, skyquality, skybrightness, skytemperature
Park sensorsUDP broadcast (Dark Dragons park-sensor JSON, port 23435), found automatically; or Alpaca SafetyMonitor per sensor
RigsNINA Advanced API on each rig (sequence, target, frame count, guiding, HFR); mount from Alpaca Telescope if there's no NINA. The display remembers each rig's last state, so it can show Finished or Lost after NINA has gone away

Settings, as on CloudRoll

  • Find Alpaca devices button: discovery on UDP 32227 lists every device on the network; tick the ones to show and give each a short name.
  • Each device entry: host, port, type, device number, label. Pick one SafetyMonitor as the safety verdict and, optionally, one rain sensor for wet or dry.
  • Park and safety sensors on the UDP broadcast appear by themselves; rename or hide them.
  • Rigs: name plus NINA host and port (Advanced API plugin, default 1888). Optional Alpaca Telescope per rig. More than two rigs page two at a time, like the WT32's footer.
  • NINA only runs while imaging, so a rig that doesn't answer is Inactive (grey), not an error. After a run, its final state stays on screen as Finished until a set time (default 12:00), then goes back to Inactive. If NINA stops answering in the middle of a sequence, the rig shows as Lost (red) until that same time.
  • Poll every 15 s while the screen is shown, every 60 s otherwise; anything not heard from in 3 polls shows as offline.
